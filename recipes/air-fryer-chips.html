Air Fryer Chips

Description

Golden crispy chips, cooked in the air fryer so they’ve got a nice bit of crunch, but they’re soft in the middle.

Ingredients

  • 700 g (1 1/2 lbs) of floury potatoes
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp frying oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika

Steps

  1. Peel and slice potatoes into chips (about 1cm thick)
  2. If they're a bit grubby, then rinse in cold water, drain and pat dry, if they're not grubby you don't need to do this step.
  3. Add the chips to a bowl and toss with the cornflour until coated
  4. Add oil, salt, pepper, and paprika and toss again so they're evenly coated
  5. Place in the air fryer and cook 190C/375F for 20 mins, shaking every 5 minutes
  6. Serve with a sprinkling of salt and pepper if you like.
