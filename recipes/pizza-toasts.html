Pizza Toasts

Description

Perfect for when you need a quick cheesy-carb fix, these pizza toasts are simple and easy to get on the table

Ingredients

  • 4 thick slices of bread
  • 1 tablespoon tomato puree
  • 3 tablespoons tomato ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon dried oregano
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder¼ teaspoon garlic powder
  • 150 g (1 ½ packed cups) grated mixed cheese
  • 4 cherry tomatoes
  • 6 slices pepperoni
  • 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano (to serve)
  • 2 spring onions

Steps

  1. Lightly toast the bread on both sides under the grill
  2. Mix together the tomato puree, tomato ketchup, olive oil, oregano and garlic powder in a small bowl.
  3. Top the toast slices with the sauce and spread to the edges.
  4. Sprinkle on the grated cheese, then arrange the tomato slices and pepperoni pieces on top.
  5. Place back under the grill (broiler) on a high heat, until the cheese is melted and bubbling (about 3-5 minutes).
  6. Serve topped with a sprinkling of dried oregano and spring onions.
